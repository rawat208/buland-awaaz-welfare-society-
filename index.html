Our Vision

To build an Educated, Empowered and environmentally Responsible society where communities come together to create a sustainable and inclusive future.

Our Mission

To design and implement community-oriented initiatives in the areas of Education, employment awareness and Environmental protection, while encouraging active citizen participation and sustainable practices.

About Us

Who we are

Established on 2 October 2015, Buland Awaaz Welfare Society is a social organization committed to creating positive and sustainable change in society. Founded by Shri Kuldeep Hindustani, the organization works for the betterment of underprivileged communities, promotes opportunities and contributes towards environmental protection.

We believe education creates empowerment, employment creates economic independence, and environmental responsibility creates a sustainable future — so our programs are built around awareness, participation, collaboration and action at the grassroots level.

01

Education for Everyone

We believe every child deserves access to education and the opportunity to build a better future. We support initiatives that promote educational awareness and opportunities for underprivileged communities.

02

Fight Against Unemployment

We contribute towards economic empowerment by encouraging skill development, employment awareness, entrepreneurship and opportunities for sustainable livelihoods.

03

Protect the Environment

We promote environmental responsibility through awareness campaigns, sustainable alternatives, waste reduction and initiatives aimed at reducing plastic pollution.

Become a Volunteer

Your time. Your skills. Your impact.

Volunteering is an opportunity to turn your concern for society into meaningful action. Volunteers help us reach communities, organize awareness activities, support environmental campaigns and spread our message to a wider audience.

Volunteer with us in:

Education & Community Outreach Environmental Awareness Plastic Reduction Campaigns Kapda Thaila Bank Activities Bio Carry Bags Awareness School & College Programs Community Events Digital & Social Media Awareness Event Coordination Documentation & Communication Fundraising & Partnerships

Who can volunteer?
Anyone who believes in creating positive social change — students, professionals, entrepreneurs, homemakers, senior citizens and community members.

Why volunteer? Learn. Connect. Contribute. Lead.
Volunteering gives you an opportunity to develop new skills, meet like-minded people and contribute directly to causes that matter.

Your contribution may be small, but together our collective impact can be transformational.
